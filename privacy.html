Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

HANI Consultancy Services ("HANI Loans", "we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect through haniloans.in, our social media pages, lead forms and WhatsApp, and how we use it.

1. Data we collect

Name, mobile number, city, loan type, employment type, income range, required loan amount, repayment / settlement history you choose to share, and documents you send us for a loan application. We also receive basic technical data (such as the ad or link you came from).

2. Why we use it

  • To call or WhatsApp you about your loan enquiry.
  • To check which banks / NBFCs may suit your profile.
  • To prepare and submit your loan application, with your consent.
  • To follow up on your application and for our internal records.

3. Sharing

We share your details only with banks, NBFCs and our corporate DSA partners for processing your loan application, and with service providers who help us run our systems. We do not sell your personal data.

4. Storage and security

Your data is stored in access-controlled systems. Only authorised team members can see it. We keep it only as long as needed for your enquiry, application and legal requirements.

5. Your choices

You can ask us to stop calling you, to correct your data, or to delete it (unless we must keep it by law). Contact us using the details below. You can withdraw your consent at any time.
