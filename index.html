Frequently Asked Questions

Is HANI Loans a bank?

No. HANI Loans (HANI Consultancy Services) is a loan consultancy / DSA. We help you find a suitable bank or NBFC and support you through the process. The loan is given by the lender.

What loan amounts do you arrange?

From ₹50,000 up to ₹50 crore, depending on the loan type, your profile and the lender's policy.

Do you guarantee loan approval?

No. Approval, loan amount, interest rate and terms are decided only by the bank / NBFC based on your profile and documents. We help you apply where your chances are best.

What documents are needed for a Personal Loan?

Usually PAN, Aadhaar, latest 3 months salary slips and 6 months bank statement. The lender may ask for more.

What documents are needed for a Business Loan?

Usually KYC (PAN, Aadhaar), business proof for 3+ years, 12 months bank statement, and ITR / GST if available. The lender may ask for more.

Are there any charges?

Checking your eligibility is free. Any consultancy charges, if applicable, are explained clearly before you proceed. Lender processing fees are as per the lender.

Do you serve my city?

We serve customers across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana.
